About me

Hi, i'm Jovan.

I'm a product designer who thrives in the messy, ambiguous early stages of product development - where strategy, user needs, and business goals need to find their balance. That's where thoughtful design can make the biggest impact.

Over the years, I've worked in startups, agencies, and enterprise environments. That range taught me to adapt quickly, lead confidently, and think both big-picture and down to the pixel.

I collaborate best with teams that value curiosity, clear thinking, and honest communication. I'm also deeply invested in creating design processes that scale, especially in fast-moving or growing teams.

When I'm not designing, I'm probably playing drums or guitar, exploring new tools, or building small side projects just to see what breaks.

BoatHelper - Boat Service Marketplace

A mobile app that connects boat owners with local craftsmen for repairs, maintenance, and services.

The app covers the full service loop: browsing and finding craftsmen, tracking open tasks and offers, managing multiple boats, and paying for completed work with an integrated review flow. I designed the product end to end - from information architecture through high-fidelity, interaction-ready screens - translating a client brief and use-case list into a cohesive mobile experience.
